Contact the responsible body

There are two parts to contacting the responsible body. First, an informal phone call. Then, formal letters are sent by email.

Initial phone call

You should decide in your RISE team who is best placed to contact the responsible body about this school.


Who should make initial contact

The person who should contact the responsible depends on the circumstances of the school and responsible body, and their relationship with DfE.

Depending on the type of school, it would be one of the trust relationship manager, the local authorty lead or a member of the RISE team.

Discuss what the best approach is for this responsible body as a team and make your decision based on your collective knowledge.

There is guidance on SharePoint (opens in new tab) that contains links to a call script and answers to commonly asked questions that can help you.

What to say on the call

During the call, reassure the responsible body that RISE targeted intervention is a collaborative process.

Explain that the RISE team and adviser will work together with the responsible body and school and their experience, plans and views will be taken into account.

Formal letters of notification

You must send a formal letter, by email, to the responsible body to inform them the school is entering the targeted intervention programme.

The email must be sent from the regional RISE team mailbox.

You must include the enrolment letter and RISE targeted intervention information sheet
The formal letter must be sent to particular people at the responsible body depending on the type of school

Who to send the email to

Academies

Send the email to the Chair of the trust and the trust CEO.

Local authority maintained schools

Send the email to the Chair of governors, the director of children's services at the local authority. You must also copy in the school's headteacher.

Faith schools

Copy in the diocese or other relevant religious authority if it is a faith school.

Foundation schools

Copy in the foundation trust if it is a foundation school.

DfE

You must copy in the regional director or their support team, along with the trust relationship manager or local authority lead.
