Request improvement grant offer letter

Email the grant team at rise.grant@education.gov.uk and ask them to send the improvement grant offer letter to the school.

Make sure you add the contacts the grant team need to this project.
Contacts needed

School

You must add the contact details for the school's chief accounting officer, or equivalent.

For academies this is usually the CEO of the trust. For local authority maintained schools, this is usually the headteacher.

You must include their:

  • name
  • email address
  • postal address

Contact the grant team if you're not sure who the chief accounting officer is.

Supporting organisation

You must also include the contact details of the supporting organisation's responsible body.

If the supporting organisation is an academy trust, you must include the name and email address of its chief accounting officer. This is usually its CEO.

If the supporting organisation is a local authority maintained school, you must include the name and email address of the headteacher.

The RISE grant team will use this to check the costed activities.
You may need to copy in the regional director's office rather than them personally.
