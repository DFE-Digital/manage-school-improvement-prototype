Escalate an engagement concern

If you have been unable resolve an engagement concern, you may need to use mandation to compel the school to undergo the intervention.

Before you start

In order to escalate an engagement concern, you must have:

  1. used any relationship levers available to the department
  2. approval to escalate from a regional director
  3. ratification from SOPU (Schools Operational Practice Unit)

Gather evidence

Enter details of any contentious or complicated issues. This can include past or present legal action, political affiliations and conflicts of interest.

  • adviser notes of meetings
  • your contact log
